# Preserve concurrent work and design evidence
Inspect Git status and preserve unrelated and concurrent work. Preserve the existing research/design procedures and their evidence unless the authorized task calls for changing them. Keep credentials, private correspondence, personal machine paths and actual consumer data out of tracked content.

Do not hard-wrap prose or introduce unrelated reformatting. Verify relative links, affected resources and audience suitability before a checkpoint. Preserve the distinction between agreed direction, proposals, technical verification and actual human adoption; a successful check is evidence of its covered properties, not permission or agreement.
